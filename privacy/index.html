Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

Chipmunk Challenge does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (levels cleared, coins, hearts, daily streak, and preferences such as background and haptics) on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device, except as part of your own iPhone backups to iCloud or your computer, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Network access

The game does not connect to the internet and does not send data to us or to anyone else.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online features are added, this page will be updated before that version is released.
