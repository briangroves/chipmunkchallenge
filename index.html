Chipmunk Challenge

Tap nests, clear the picture.

A picture puzzle for iPhone

Every board is a picture made of colored blocks. Tap a nest to send its chipmunks to a holding slot, where they munch through the blocks of their color. Slots are limited, so think a few moves ahead.

Play through the levels, take on a new Daily Challenge every day, and mine coins on bonus boards.
